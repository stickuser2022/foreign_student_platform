Undergraduate Programs of Academy of Arts & Design

Formerly known as the Central Academy of Arts & Design, the Academy merged with Tsinghua University in 1999. With a prestigious heritage dating back to 1956, the Academy of Arts & Design offers a comprehensive range of disciplines, state-of-the-art teaching laboratories, and advanced research infrastructure, and it enjoys an outstanding reputation both nationally and internationally.


Currently, the Academy comprises 10 departments offering 20 undergraduate programs. It hosts three first-level arts disciplines—Design, Fine Arts, and Art Theory—all authorized to confer doctoral degrees. The Academy is supported by a highly qualified faculty, including numerous internationally acclaimed practitioners and thought leaders recognized at the forefront of their fields locally and globally.


The Academy maintains academic partnerships with over 60 leading institutions across the United States, Britain, France, Australia, Japan, and other countries. Every year, dozens of prominent domestic and international scholars are invited to teach courses in China.


The Tsinghua Arts and Design Institute in Milan (ADIM), officially established on April 17, 2018, serves as a strategic European hub for transcultural design education to cultivate international talent. By integrating global educational resources, ADIM offers students interdisciplinary, cross-regional, and cross-cultural learning, researching, and internship opportunities, cultivating innovative talents with global competence to address the challenges of globalization.


Tsinghua University is committed to equipping students with a solid knowledge foundation, academic excellence, good personal qualities, innovative thinking, global vision, as well as a strong sense of mission and social responsibility, helping students achieve their full potential through self-development. In line with these goals, the Academy of Arts & Design continuously advances educational reforms to cultivate innovative artistic talent with international perspectives, ambitious dreams, practical skills to meet the needs of the times, and a strong sense of responsibility. Through these efforts, the Academy strives to establish itself as a world-renowned institution in fine arts and design.
