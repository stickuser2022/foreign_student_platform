Application Procedures

1. Online Application

1.1  Create an account in the Online Application System.

1.2  Complete the application form within the designated application periods.

1.3  Upload all documents as required.

1.4  Pay the non-refundable application fee (800 RMB) online and submit the application.


2. Comprehensive Evaluation

2.1  Document Screening

Applications that are complete and accurate will be reviewed by Tsinghua University.

2.2  Comprehensive Assessment

2.2.1 Applicants who pass the Document Screening will be invited to participate in the Comprehensive Assessment. The method and schedule of the Comprehensive Assessment will be informed in advance.

2.2.2 For applicants to the Arts Division (Design & Fine Arts) or the Architecture Division, a portfolio review will be required.

2.2.3 Applicants to the programs in the Arts Division will be notified separately regarding the specific assessment arrangements.


3. Admission Decision and Offer

Admission decisions are made based on a holistic evaluation of academic performance, language proficiency, supporting materials, and the results of the Comprehensive Assessment.
