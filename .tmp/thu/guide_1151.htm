Tsinghua University Zijing College: Global Talents in Science and Engineering Program Admission Guide 2026

2025-09-30

Tsinghua University Zijing College

Global Talents in Science and Engineering Program Admission Guide 2026


Zijing College is Tsinghua University’s first undergraduate residential college, offering fully English-taught programs. It is developed to enhance the university’s international outreach and expand horizons in open education . Leveraging Tsinghua’s strengths in engineering, Zijing College creates an eco-system of global readiness that embraces an international cast of faculty, diverse peer-learning experiences, an immersive English-speaking environment, and global exposure opportunities.Zijing College pioneers a new pathway of STEAM education that integrates AI, Art and Architecture into STEM and emphasises AI fundamentals, design thinking, and aesthetic literacy. By encouraging internships with international organisations and corporations through Tsinghua’s fast-expanding international connections, Zijing College aims to cultivate top engineering talents with strong global competency.

The Global Talents in Science and Engineering Program is a top-tier undergraduate engineering program designed for outstanding students worldwide. The program aims to cultivate global leaders with creative engineering vision, cross-cultural organizational wisdom, and interdisciplinary collaboration skills; a community of learners driven by cross-cultural and cross-disciplinary explorations; and future practitioners for innovation and creation. It brings together Tsinghua University's excellent faculty and integrates cutting-edge engineering education concepts from around the world, offering comprehensive academic and practical education.

1. Program Highlights

Students can personalise their learning based on the multi-disciplinary curriculum that integrates information science technology (IST), mechanical engineering (ME), civil engineering (CE) and design science (DS), and obtain Bachelor of Engineering in Interdisciplinary Engineering or Bachelor of Engineering in traditional majors upon successfully meeting degree requirements.

Students will receive rigorous training in fields such as Interdisciplinary Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Automation, Electronic Information Science and Technology, Mechanical Engineering, Measurement, Control Technology and Instruments, Vehicle Engineering, Civil Engineering and Systems, Traffic Engineering, Architecture, Urban Planning, and Landscape Architecture. Through rich cultural experiences and interdisciplinary learning, the program focuses on enhancing students' abilities to solve complex engineering problems. Students will develop exceptional skills in engineering design, communication, teamwork, scientific experimentation, as well as lifelong learning, enabling them to play critical roles in global science and engineering fields.

1.1 All instructions of this program are in English.

Those who have completed the required courses and passed all assessments and final thesis defense will be awarded bachelor's degree by Tsinghua University.

1.2 Program duration:

Four academic years.

1.3 Education Objectives:

Students will be grounded in a profound belief in the shared future for humanity and become individuals with cultivated integrity and nurtured vigour in body and mind.

Students will be empowered to customise their learning based on the multi-disciplinary curriculum that integrates information science and technology (IST), mechanical engineering (ME), civil engineering (CE), and design science (DS) to develop their own knowledge system.

Upon graduation, students will become blueprint drafter who can harness creative engineering minds to conceive groundbreaking solutions; culture weavers who can translate knowledge across divides to unite global teams; master synthesisers who can ground vision in action to unravel complex engineering challenges; and leaders of innovations who can compete on the world stage while building collaborative bridges across pioneering horizons.

2. Eligibility

2.1 Foreign citizens, in good physical and mental health, friendly to China, and awarded a certificate of graduation from high school.

2.2 Academic performance

Outstanding academic performance. Proof should be provided through (but not limited to) the following document(s):

2.2.1 High school/University (if applicable) transcripts (in Chinese or English).

2.2.2 Certificate of National/Regional Graduation Examination/Matriculation Examination.

2.2.3 Results of international standard tests (e.g. SAT/ACT/A-Level/AP/IB/CSCA/others).

2.2.4 Other Supporting Materials.

2.3 Language proficiency

Applicants must have a high level of English language proficiency. For those with a native language other than English, a test report of English language proficiency will need to be provided (e.g. TOEFL/IELTS/others).

2.4 Aged 18 or above (by September 1, 2026). For the applicants under 18, please bring the relevant supporting documents to the official registration.

2.5 The conditions set by the Document No. 12 (2020) of the Ministry of Education of the PRC should be met.

3. Application Procedures

3.1 Online Application

3.1.1 Create your account in the Online Application System.

3.1.2 Complete the application form during the application period.

3.1.3 Upload the documents as required (see “List of Uploading Documents in the Online Application System” for more details).

3.1.4 Pay the application fee (800 RMB) online and submit the application.

3.2 Comprehensive Evaluation

3.2.1 Application Review

Applications submitted with all the information correct and completed will be evaluated by Tsinghua University.

3.2.2 Integrative Test

Applicants who have passed the Application Review will be admitted to the integrative test. The method and time of the integrative test will be informed in advance.

3.3 Admission Decision and Offer

The admission decision is based on a comprehensive evaluation of academic performance, language ability, supporting materials as well as integrative tests.

4. Schedule

Online Application Period (Beijing Time): 8:00 AM September 30, 2025 – 5:00 PM February 28, 2026

Results will be issued by: Early May, 2026

Note: The method and time of each round’s integrative test will be informed in advance.

5.2 Accommodation

Zijing College is a residential college where students will be accommodated in designated buildings on campus. Online reservations must be made within the designated time (availability of rooms is subject to the booking system). For accommodation fees and other details, please refer to the Tsinghua University International Student and Scholar Center website.

6. Admission

Applicants who are granted admission will receive their admission documents from Tsinghua University in around June 2026.

7. Scholarship

To better support and assist international undergraduate students in their development at Tsinghua, the university actively promotes international exchange and cooperation. In accordance with relevant policies, Tsinghua continuously seeks donations from overseas alumni and corporations to support the academic development and personal growth of international students.
