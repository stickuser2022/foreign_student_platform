Scholarships Application Information
一、申请Application
中国政府奖学金-国别双边项目 Chinese Government Scholarship-Bilateral Program 
申请时间2025年12月初至2026年5月。
The regular application period runs from early December 2025 to May 2026.
请直接与本国留学生派遣部门联系（一般为中国驻当地使领馆）。具体申请要求请查询中国国家留学生基金委网站http://www.csc.edu.cn/studyinchina或http://www.campuschina.org，以及武汉大学网站https://admission.whu.edu.cn/。
Please apply directly to the dispatching authorities for overseas study in your home country (usually the local Chinese Embassy or Consulate). For more details, please visit: http://www.csc.edu.cn/studyinchina or http://www.campuschina.org, and the Wuhan University website at https://admission.whu.edu.cn/.
国际中文教师奖学金 International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship
申请时间2026年3月初至2026年5月初。
The regular application period runs from early March to early May 2026.
申请者需登录http://www.chinese.cn进行申请，同时还需登录武汉大学申请网站https://admission.whu.edu.cn/进行申请。
Please complete the online application through http://www.chinese.cn, and also complete the online application through https://admission.whu.edu.cn/ .
欧盟之窗奖学金EU Window Chinese Government Scholarship 
申请时间为2025年12月至2026年2月。
The application period runs from December 2025 to February 2026.
该项目面向非中国籍的欧盟成员国优秀学生，支持其来武汉大学完成本科、硕士、博士或进修项目的学习。招生简章和专业介绍等均在“留学中国”网站发布，请同时登www.campuschina.org以及武汉大学网站https://admission.whu.edu.cn/查询具体信息并完成申请。
This program is designed to provide assistance to outstanding students from EU member states to complete their undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral or advanced studies at Wuhan University. Please visit http://www.campuschina.org and https://admission.whu.edu.cn/ for detailed information and submit the required documentation on both websites. 
沙特阿拉伯国王奖学金 Saudi Arabia Government Scholarship
申请时间为2025年11月1日至2026年5月30日。
The application period runs from Nov. 1, 2025, to May 30, 2026.
该项目面向沙特阿拉伯籍优秀学生，支持其来武汉大学完成本科项目的学习。请登录武汉大学网站https://admission.whu.edu.cn/获得更多信息并完成网上申请。也可咨询沙特阿拉伯驻北京文化处获得相关信息。优先考虑已进行预科学习的申请者。
This program is designed to provide assistance to outstanding students from Saudi Arabia to complete their undergraduate studies at Wuhan University. Please apply directly through https://admission.whu.edu.cn/. For more detailed information, please consult the Saudi Arabia Cultural Mission in Beijing. Applicants who already complete the foundation course will be in processing soon.  
留学武大——学历/进修生新生奖学金 Studying in Wuhan University – Academic/ Non-degree Students Freshman Scholarship
在提交其他申请材料时同时提交申请。
The application will be open during submitting other needing files.
该项目面向自费来华留学的优秀新生，支持其来武汉大学完成本科学习。请登录武汉大学网站https://admission.whu.edu.cn/获得更多信息并完成网上申请。
This program is designed to provide assistance to outstanding freshmen who study at their own expenses to complete theirundergraduate studies at Wuhan University. Please visit https://admission.whu.edu.cn/ for detailed information and submit the required documentation on website.

三、报到时间Registration Period
来华留学生新生注册报到时间为每年8月至9月，具体请看录取通知书。 
The usual registration period for international students is from August to September. Please check the Admission Notice for the exact registration time. 
报到时需携齐全的报到材料，包括个人普通护照原件、录取通知书、签证申请表复印件（JW202/JW201）及入境学生签证。不符合入学条件者，取消入学资格。
When registering at Wuhan University, admitted students must provide their ordinary passport, Copy of Admission Notice, Copy of Visa Application Form (JW202/JW201) and student visa. Otherwise, the admission will be considered invalid.
