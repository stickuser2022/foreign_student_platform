     Schedule 
     
     
   1.First Round Application and Admission    
   &nbsp;* Design/Fine Arts programs in Arts Division are open ONLY in this round   
  Please see “ 2026 Undergraduate Programs of the Academy of Arts &amp; Design (Admission for International Applicants) ” for details.  
 
  
   
    Online application (Beijing Time): 
    8:00 AM September 30, 2025—5:00 PM November 28, 2025 
   
   
    The First Round result will be issued by: 
    January, 2026 
   
  
 
     
   2. Second Round Application and Admission     
 
  
   
    Online application (Beijing Time): 
    8:00 AM November 29, 2025—5:00 PM February 28, 2026 
   
   
    The Second Round result will be issued by: 
    Early May ,  2026
