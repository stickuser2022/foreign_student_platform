2026InternationalOrganizationsandGlobal
Governance（IOGG）（English-taught）
Program Overview
The International Organizations and Global Governance
undergraduate program is a four-year Bachelor of Laws degree taught
entirely in English. It focuses on the theoretical foundations and practical
development of global governance, international organizations, and
international relations, equipping students with the skills and knowledge
needed to engage with the complexities of the global system.
Mission
The program is committed to cultivating well-rounded individuals
who excel in moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic, and labor
development. Its goal is to provide students with a solid theoretical
foundation and practical capabilities, enabling them to systematically
master the theories and practices of global governance and international
organizations, understand international rules and norms, and develop the
capacity to analyze and solve complex global issues.
Vision
We aspire to nurture graduates with a global outlook, innovative
thinking, professional ethics, and strong social responsibility. Students
will be encouraged to become independent critical thinkers and lifelong
learners, capable of interdisciplinary collaboration, leadership, and
meaningful contribution to global society.

advanced academic research.

2026BachelorofMedicineandBachelor
ofSurgery(MBBS)(English-taught)
Program Overview
To promote international education, Fudan University began enrolling
international students in the English-taught undergraduate medical
program in fall 2008. The program aims to nurture high-caliber medical
graduates with solid medical knowledge, competent clinical skills, and
professionalism, while giving due consideration to the possible needs for
them to serve in countries outside China. Graduates of the program will
have the ability to work in medical and health institutions, making
meaningful contributions to the areas of patient care, education, and
research.
The program follows the principles of consolidating academic
knowledge, with emphasis on technical skills, interdisciplinary studies,
and clinical practice. Excellent students will have the opportunity to
participate in overseas clinical internship programs. After completing the
required number of credits, students will be conferred a diploma from
Fudan University, and qualified students will receive a degree of
Bachelor of Medicine and the Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS). Graduates
will have the opportunity to register for medical licensing exams in
accordance with the requirements of the relevant regulatory bodies of the
countries in which they choose to practice.

Program Overview
Offered by School of Data Science (SDS) of Fudan, UIPDB is a
four-year full-time undergraduate program of Data Science and Big Data
Technology with a focus on data analytics and technology applications. It
provides students with a comprehensive background in statistics,
computer science, mathematics and applied disciplines related to big data,
together with an in-depth understanding of interdisciplinary studies and
artificial intelligence. Elective courses are offered in three specialized
modules: Statistics and Analysis, Systems and Data Mining, and Data
Science for Social Science.
Our graduates would be well equipped to accept the positions with
leading internet companies, financial institutions, as well as to be
employed in government departments. This major is also fully prepared
for graduate studies, such as in statistics, computer science, business
analytics, or medical analysis.
